Battery warning
If the red battery warning lamp remains illuminated while the engine is
running, the charging system may not be charging the battery correctly. Turn
off non-essential electrical equipment and arrange a check by a qualified
service technician. If the vehicle behaves unexpectedly, stop in a safe place.

Tyre pressure warning
The tyre pressure warning lamp means one or more tyres may be below the
recommended pressure. When it is safe, inspect all tyres and compare their
pressures with the values on the vehicle's tyre information label. Do not rely
on the warning lamp as a substitute for regular tyre inspection.

Engine temperature warning
If the engine temperature warning appears, pull over safely as soon as
possible and switch off the engine. Do not open the coolant reservoir while it
is hot. Contact a qualified service technician if the warning remains or
coolant is leaking.
